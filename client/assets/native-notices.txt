Visual Pinball Android - integrated in GameClub 0.10.0
Unmodified native components from the official BGFX mobile release:
https://github.com/vpinball/vpinball/tree/af26b2d9392b4c9ec3e3edff4bbd69562085a44c
Exact APK and component SHA-256 registry: client/platform-config/pinball_runtime.json.
The upstream Visual Pinball license is included at assets/pinball/licenses/VisualPinball.txt.
Most Visual Pinball sources are GPL-3.0-or-later; files not yet relicensed retain
the historical MAME-derived terms described in that upstream notice.
PinMAME source and licensing: https://github.com/vpinball/pinmame
Other upstream native components retain their own licenses, including SDL_image,
SDL_ttf, FreeImage, FFmpeg, Wine VBScript, AltSound, DOF, DMDUtil, Serum, VNI,
PUPDMD, sockpp, ZeDMD and libzip. Their upstream build and dependency sources:
https://github.com/vpinball/vpinball/tree/af26b2d9392b4c9ec3e3edff4bbd69562085a44c/standalone
GameClub's Android adapter is client/native/pinball_android.cpp and
client/platform-config/PinballActivity.kt. No commercial tables or ROMs are included.
